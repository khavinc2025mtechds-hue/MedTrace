# Project overview

MedTrace AI is a B2B research prototype for medical-device manufacturers, quality engineers, complaint investigators and regulatory affairs teams. Infusion pumps are the initial scope. It connects a new complaint to extracted facts, historical FDA narratives, descriptive patterns, candidate recalls and cited regulatory text, then stores a draft investigation for human review.

Included: a runnable local dashboard/API, a real-data development corpus, advanced training/retrieval implementations, evaluation scripts and eight notebooks. Not included: independently reviewed research labels, trained task-specific weights, validated performance claims, or a production compliance certification.

The contribution is an integrated traceable investigation workflow. A literature novelty claim requires a verified comparison with the selected papers; do not claim that no prior system combines these techniques.
